About Me

My name is Kristina Cook, and I am building my skills in web development. This portfolio shows the work I have done while learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

My Professional Goals

My main goal in learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript is to contribute to my workplace website. Right now, when something on our site needs to be updated or stops working, I have to wait for someone else to fix it. I want to understand how the site is built so I can make those updates myself.

As my skills grow, I want to become the person my team can count on to solve website issues as they come up. That means being able to find the cause of a problem, like a broken layout, a missing image, or a link that doesn't work, and fix it quickly and correctly.

In the long term, I hope to go beyond fixing problems and help improve the website overall. I want to make it easier for visitors to find what they need, keep it looking consistent and professional, and make sure it works well on every device.
